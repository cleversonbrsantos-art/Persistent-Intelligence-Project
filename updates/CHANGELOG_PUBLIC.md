# Public Changelog

## Purpose

This document tracks major public milestones in the evolution of the Cortana research project.

It focuses on research progress, vision, and publicly disclosed achievements.

Implementation details, proprietary systems, internal architectures, and experimental mechanisms are intentionally omitted.

---

# 2026

## Research Foundation

The public research initiative for Cortana was formally organized and documented.

Core areas of investigation were defined:

* Persistent Memory
* Long-Term Continuity
* Cognitive Reflection
* Responsible Adaptation
* Human-Centered Intelligence
* Family-Oriented AI
* Cognitive Safety

---

## Public Documentation

The first public documentation set was established.

Published documents include:

* Vision
* Research
* Safety
* Ethics
* Roadmap
* Public Architecture
* FAQ
* Demonstration Policy

The objective is to improve transparency while protecting ongoing research.

---

## Accessibility Commitment

The project formally adopted a local-first philosophy.

Research continues to explore how meaningful cognitive systems can remain accessible without requiring enterprise-scale infrastructure.

---

## Safety Framework

Safety, privacy, information integrity, and human oversight were established as permanent research priorities.

Future capabilities are expected to evolve alongside corresponding safety research.

---

## Long-Term Direction

The project reaffirmed its long-term objective:

> To explore whether persistent cognitive intelligence can become more useful, more responsible, and more human-centered through continuity, memory, and safe adaptation.

Future entries will document significant public milestones, research directions, demonstrations, publications, and major project developments.
