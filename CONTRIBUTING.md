# Contributing

## Thank You

Thank you for your interest in the Cortana research project.

Cortana is an ongoing investigation into persistent cognitive intelligence, memory, continuity, safety, and long-term human-AI interaction.

Constructive feedback, discussion, and responsible collaboration are always appreciated.

---

## Current Project Status

Cortana is currently developed as an independent research initiative.

At this stage, the public repository primarily serves as a platform for:

* Research documentation
* Public transparency
* Project updates
* Community discussion
* Long-term vision sharing

The repository is not currently accepting direct code contributions.

---

## How You Can Contribute

Even without direct code contributions, there are many ways to help:

### Research Discussion

Contribute ideas, questions, and perspectives related to:

* Persistent memory
* Long-term AI interaction
* Cognitive safety
* Human-centered AI
* Privacy
* Learning governance
* Family-oriented AI

---

### Documentation Feedback

Help improve:

* Clarity
* Accuracy
* Readability
* Accessibility

Constructive feedback is always welcome.

---

### Ethical and Safety Review

One of the most valuable forms of contribution is critical review of:

* Safety principles
* Ethical considerations
* Privacy approaches
* Long-term risks

Thoughtful criticism helps strengthen the project.

---

### Testing and Demonstrations

Future public demonstrations may provide opportunities for:

* User feedback
* Experience reports
* Safety observations
* Usability suggestions

---

## Research Integrity

Not all aspects of the project are publicly disclosed.

Certain implementation details, architectures, experimental systems, and ongoing research components remain private.

This approach exists to:

* Protect research integrity
* Protect intellectual property
* Prevent premature disclosure
* Allow responsible experimentation

---

## Community Expectations

Contributors and community members are expected to:

* Remain respectful
* Engage constructively
* Avoid harassment
* Respect privacy
* Encourage responsible discussion

Healthy disagreement is welcome.

Personal attacks are not.

---

## Feature Requests

Feature requests may be considered when they align with:

* Project goals
* Safety principles
* Research priorities
* Long-term vision

Not every suggestion will be adopted.

---

## Security Issues

Please report security concerns through the procedures described in SECURITY.md.

Do not publicly disclose vulnerabilities before responsible review.

---

## Final Note

Cortana is ultimately a research project.

The goal is not simply to build software.

The goal is to better understand how persistent cognitive intelligence can be developed responsibly, safely, and in service of human needs.

Thank you for helping move that conversation forward.
