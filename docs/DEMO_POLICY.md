# Demonstration Policy

## Purpose

Public demonstrations of Cortana exist to showcase research progress, gather feedback, and improve understanding of persistent cognitive intelligence.

Demonstrations should not be interpreted as final implementations, final capabilities, or completed research outcomes.

The project remains under active development.

---

## Experimental Nature

Cortana is an experimental research platform.

Capabilities, behaviors, interfaces, and research directions may evolve as new findings emerge.

Users should expect ongoing refinement and change.

---

## Safety First

Safety takes priority over capability.

Experimental features may be restricted, modified, paused, or removed whenever necessary to preserve user safety, research integrity, or system stability.

---

## Privacy

Users should avoid sharing:

* Personal identification data
* Financial information
* Passwords
* Medical records
* Sensitive personal information

Public demonstrations are intended for research, testing, and educational purposes.

---

## Memory and Context

Demonstration environments may use limited, temporary, or experimental forms of memory.

Public demonstrations should not be considered representative of the project's full research objectives.

Memory behavior may vary depending on the research environment.

---

## User Expectations

Cortana should not be considered:

* A medical system
* A legal advisor
* A financial advisor
* A replacement for professional services
* A replacement for human relationships

Users remain responsible for their own decisions and actions.

---

## Respectful Interaction

Users are expected to interact respectfully.

Attempts to intentionally manipulate, abuse, degrade, or misuse demonstration environments may result in restrictions or removal of access.

---

## Child Safety

Future family-oriented deployments will require additional safeguards.

Protection of children, vulnerable users, and family environments remains a high-priority research objective.

---

## Feedback

Constructive feedback is encouraged.

Research benefits from diverse perspectives, testing scenarios, and responsible community participation.

---

## Availability

Demonstration environments may be modified, paused, replaced, or removed as research priorities evolve.

Availability is not guaranteed.

---

## Final Principle

The purpose of public access is research, education, transparency, and learning.

Demonstrations exist to help explore the future of persistent cognitive intelligence in a responsible and human-centered way.
