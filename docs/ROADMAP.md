# Roadmap

## Cortana Research Roadmap

Cortana follows a cognitive research roadmap rather than a traditional feature roadmap.

The objective is not simply to add functionality.

The objective is to better understand how persistent cognitive systems can evolve responsibly over time.

---

## Phase I — Persistence

### Objective

Establish continuity beyond individual conversations.

### Research Focus

* Context preservation
* Historical continuity
* Long-term interaction support
* Persistent user experience

### Status

Active

---

## Phase II — Memory

### Objective

Explore how meaningful information can be preserved over time.

### Research Focus

* Memory persistence
* Information retrieval
* Context reconstruction
* Long-term knowledge preservation

### Status

Active

---

## Phase III — Reflection

### Objective

Study whether accumulated experiences can be analyzed to improve long-term usefulness.

### Research Focus

* Pattern recognition
* Experience analysis
* Context refinement
* Longitudinal consistency

### Status

Research

---

## Phase IV — Responsible Adaptation

### Objective

Investigate safe approaches to long-term learning and adaptation.

### Research Focus

* Learning governance
* Information quality
* Stability preservation
* Human oversight

### Status

Research

---

## Phase V — Personal Context

### Objective

Improve understanding of long-term user context while respecting privacy and autonomy.

### Research Focus

* Preference awareness
* Goal continuity
* Context preservation
* Personalized assistance

### Status

Future Research

---

## Phase VI — Social Context

### Research Focus

Future Research

### Status

Future Research

### Objective

Explore cooperation between multiple intelligent systems while preserving user control.

---

## Phase IX — Longitudinal Intelligence

### Objective

Study intelligence as a process that evolves across years rather than isolated interactions.

### Research Focus

* Historical continuity
* Long-term adaptation
* Persistent context
* Behavioral stability

### Status

Long-Term Research

---

## Safety Roadmap

Every capability explored by Cortana should be accompanied by corresponding safety research.

Areas include:

* Cognitive safety
* Learning validation
* Privacy preservation
* Information integrity
* Human oversight
* Family safety

Capability and safety should evolve together.

---

## Guiding Principle

Cortana is not measured by the number of features it contains.

It is measured by the quality of continuity it creates.

The roadmap exists to guide research toward one central question:

> Can persistent cognitive intelligence become more useful, more responsible, and more human-centered over time?
