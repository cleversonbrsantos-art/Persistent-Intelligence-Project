# Public Architecture

## Overview

This document describes the public architectural principles behind Cortana.

It intentionally focuses on concepts rather than implementation details.

Specific algorithms, internal mechanisms, data structures, workflows, and proprietary systems are not disclosed.

The purpose of this document is to explain how the project is organized at a high level while preserving research integrity and intellectual property.

---

## Architectural Philosophy

Cortana is based on a simple principle:

> Intelligence is more than language generation.

The project explores architectures where memory, continuity, reflection, learning, safety, and reasoning interact as parts of a larger cognitive system.

External language models may contribute to reasoning and communication, but they are not treated as the entirety of intelligence.

---

## High-Level Cognitive Layers

At a conceptual level, Cortana explores interactions between several major domains:

```text
Human Interaction
        ↓
Context Processing
        ↓
Knowledge & Memory
        ↓
Reflection & Adaptation
        ↓
Response Generation
```

These domains represent research areas rather than specific implementation details.

---

## Human Interaction

The interaction layer is responsible for communication between people and the system.

Research includes:

* Context awareness
* Long-term continuity
* User assistance
* Communication quality

The objective is to support meaningful long-term interaction.

---

## Context Processing

Persistent systems require mechanisms capable of interpreting information within broader historical context.

Research areas include:

* Context preservation
* Relevance assessment
* Information prioritization
* Long-term continuity

---

## Knowledge and Memory

Memory is a central research topic within Cortana.

The project investigates how information can be preserved, organized, retrieved, and managed responsibly over time.

The objective is not infinite storage.

The objective is meaningful continuity.

---

## Reflection and Adaptation

The project explores whether accumulated experiences can contribute to long-term improvement.

Research includes:

* Pattern identification
* Context refinement
* Experience analysis
* Responsible adaptation

Adaptation is treated as a controlled process rather than unrestricted change.

---

## Safety and Governance

Safety is considered a core architectural concern.

Research areas include:

* Information integrity
* Learning governance
* Privacy preservation
* Stability maintenance
* Human oversight

Safety evolves alongside capability.

---

## External Intelligence Sources

Cortana may interact with external systems when appropriate.

These systems may contribute:

* Knowledge
* Reasoning
* Language generation
* Research assistance

The project investigates how persistent cognitive systems can cooperate with external intelligence while maintaining continuity and user-centered design.

---

## Local-First Principles

Whenever possible, the project favors approaches that:

* Preserve user ownership
* Respect privacy
* Reduce infrastructure requirements
* Remain accessible on modest hardware

Accessibility remains a long-term design objective.

---

## Research Status

The architecture remains under active development.

The concepts described here represent research directions and design principles rather than final implementations.

Many questions remain open areas of investigation.

---

## Final Principle

Cortana is not built around a single model.

The project explores how memory, continuity, learning, reflection, safety, and human interaction can work together to create more useful and responsible forms of personal AI.
