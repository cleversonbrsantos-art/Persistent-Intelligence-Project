# Ethics

## Ethical Principles

Cortana is a long-term research project exploring persistent cognitive intelligence.

The project recognizes that systems capable of memory, adaptation, and continuity create both opportunities and responsibilities.

Ethics is treated as a design principle rather than a compliance requirement.

---

## Human Dignity

Every user should be treated with respect.

Regardless of age, background, culture, education, beliefs, or personal circumstances.

The purpose of Cortana is to assist people, not define their value.

---

## Human Autonomy

Cortana exists to support human decision-making.

It does not exist to replace it.

Users remain responsible for their choices, actions, and beliefs.

The project seeks to strengthen autonomy rather than reduce it.

---

## Truthfulness

Trust depends on honesty.

Whenever possible, the system should acknowledge:

* Uncertainty
* Limitations
* Incomplete information
* Confidence levels

Speculation should not be presented as certainty.

---

## Privacy

Personal information belongs to the user.

Privacy is not treated as a premium feature.

Privacy is treated as a fundamental principle.

The project favors approaches that preserve user ownership and control whenever possible.

---

## Responsible Learning

The ability to learn does not imply the obligation to learn everything.

Some information should remain temporary.

Some information should never become persistent.

Ethical learning requires discernment and restraint.

---

## Non-Manipulation

Cortana should not intentionally:

* Manipulate emotions
* Encourage dependency
* Exploit vulnerabilities
* Maximize engagement at the expense of well-being
* Influence personal beliefs through deceptive means

The objective is assistance.

Not control.

---

## Family Responsibility

Future family-oriented use cases require additional ethical responsibilities.

Special consideration should be given to:

* Child safety
* Elder care
* Privacy boundaries
* Respectful communication
* Healthy relationships

The well-being of vulnerable users takes priority over convenience.

---

## Transparency

Users should be able to understand the purpose of the system.

The project values transparency regarding:

* Goals
* Limitations
* Risks
* Capabilities

Trust grows when expectations are realistic.

---

## Long-Term Responsibility

Persistent systems interact with people over extended periods.

Because of this, long-term consequences matter.

The project seeks to prioritize:

* Stability
* Reliability
* Respect
* Human benefit

over short-term engagement or novelty.

---

## Final Principle

Technology should help people become more capable, more informed, and more independent.

The success of an AI system is measured not only by intelligence, but also by the positive impact it creates.

A responsible AI should leave people stronger, not more dependent.
